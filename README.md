# Khushal Builders Inc. — Brand Identity Suite & High-Converting One-Pager Website

A brand identity package and modern one-pager website crafted for **Khushal Builders Inc.** based on their official promotional flyer.

---

## 🌟 What's Included

### 1. Outstanding Logo Suite
- **Photorealistic 3D Architectural Emblem** (`assets/khushal-builders-render.jpg`): Ultra-professional 3D vector-style rendering integrating the bold 'KB' monogram with architectural building tiers, gold metallic surfaces, and matte black slate.
- **Flagship Vector Logo** (`assets/logo-primary.svg`): Scalable vector graphic with the geometric KB monogram, stylized crane/excavator boom silhouette, high-contrast typography, and official tagline: *"We Transform Your Dreams Into Concrete Solutions"*.
- **Horizontal Navigation Logo** (`assets/logo-horizontal.svg`): Optimized for website headers, letterheads, invoice templates, and truck decals.
- **Official Quality Seal Stamp** (`assets/logo-badge.svg`): Circular emblem for certifications, hard-hat stickers, yard signs, and guarantee badges.
- **Favicon** (`assets/favicon.svg`): Clean high-visibility browser tab icon.
- **Brand Showcase Page** (`logo-showcase.html`): An interactive brand guide showing light/dark modes, color codes, and 1-click downloads.
